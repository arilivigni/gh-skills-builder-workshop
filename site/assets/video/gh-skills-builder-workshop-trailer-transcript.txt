What if your next onboarding guide became something people could actually practise? Turn an idea into a hands-on GitHub exercise, with GitHub Copilot and the gh-skills-builder plugin.

For educators, maintainers, enterprises, startups, and internal training, this workshop puts you in the architect's seat. Copilot helps draft the content and scaffold the files. You shape the learning experience.

One orchestrator agent and five reusable skills guide the journey: outline, bootstrap, review, publish, and orchestrate the whole loop. Install the plugin from the awesome-copilot marketplace in the Copilot app or CLI.

Start with the outcome. Define what learners will practise, what success looks like, and how to check it. Then turn the approved outline into a runnable, issue-driven GitHub Skills exercise.

The example uses Tailspin Toys to teach three practical Copilot habits: clear repository instructions, consistent quality reports, and a report-only QA agent. A small exercise, with a concrete purpose.

Checks examine what learners wrote, not just whether a file exists. Review the findings, approve a targeted fix, and review again. Useful feedback helps learners understand what to improve.

People stay in control of the decisions. Prepare the validation evidence and launch checklist, then pause. Release readiness is not permission to publish, and this workshop stops before public release.

Watch the live walkthrough, then replay the copyable prompts at your own pace. The website brings the slides, instructions and resources together. Repeatable, observable, inclusive, and human-led.
