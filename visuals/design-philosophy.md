# Signal Assembly

Signal Assembly treats intelligence as an arrangement of deliberately connected forms. A large, singular center establishes agency; smaller modular structures express capabilities without competing for identity. Space is active: the silence between components makes relationships legible. Each composition should feel meticulously crafted rather than decorated.

Color behaves as a signal rather than a spectacle. Near-black and pure white establish alternate grounds, while calibrated greens identify continuity, readiness, and permission. Soft secondary surfaces carry depth without ornamental borders. Painstaking attention to contrast lets identical visual logic survive changes in lighting and display.

Scale distinguishes intention from execution. Monumental central forms coexist with fine registration marks, precise connectors, and measured repetitions. Rhythm is deliberate but not monotonous: a compact cluster can become an expansive procession or a returning circuit. Master-level execution means every change of scale serves comprehension.

Composition makes causality tangible. Connections enter and leave defined terminals; interruptions become visible checkpoints rather than implied consent. Return paths acknowledge revision without suggesting automatic motion. The product of deep expertise is a system that reads quickly, then rewards closer attention.

Typography is sparse, essential, and integrated into the architecture. Strong sans-serif statements establish the idea, while quiet monospaced annotations identify components. No paragraph should do work that spatial organization can perform. The final work must convey countless careful refinements through alignment, breathing room, and the restraint to remove anything unnecessary.

## Typography

Mona Sans carries the voice (Display for titles, Regular for body); its companion, Hubot Sans, carries the labels and system markings.
